# Dashboard idol photo prompts (Selfmini-style, original Tathastu assets)

Style locked from competitor research (composition only — do not reuse their files on the storefront).

## Global style lock (prepend to every prompt)

```
Photorealistic ecommerce product photo, square 1:1, soft diffused studio lighting from upper-left, shallow depth of field, sharp focus on the idol, premium gift-catalog look. Cute chibi / Bal-roop miniature Hindu deity idol, smooth hand-painted finish (not rough FDM layer lines), glossy gold jewellery, round dark-wood pedestal with thin gold bead rim. Warm festive mood. No brand logos, no watermarks, no readable competitor text, no faces of people unless the shot requires anonymous hands.
```

- Keep size labels accurate: most SKUs 4 / 5 Inch; Nandi 5 / 6 / 8 Inch.
